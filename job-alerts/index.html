How SLV Job Alerts Works

1. Register using your verified mobile number.

2. Activate a free trial, monthly plan or prepaid search credit.

3. Search available job notifications through our WhatsApp Bot once launched.

4. Open official recruitment links to verify eligibility and apply.

Notifications depend on availability and verification of official sources.

Privacy Policy

When activated, SLV Job Alerts will process customer mobile numbers, account information, subscription status, payment references and job-search requests to provide the service.

Payments will be handled through an authorized payment provider. We will not store customer card details on this website.

We will use appropriate access controls and security measures. Data retention, customer support and deletion procedures will be finalized before launch.

Terms & Conditions

SLV Job Alerts is an independent job-information platform and is not affiliated with APPSC, UPSC, SSC, RRB or any government department.

Monthly subscriptions are intended to expire after 30 days without automatic renewal unless recurring payments are separately introduced with customer consent.

Customers will be able to contact the support team using the verified contact information published on this website.
