Settings

Configure electricity bill fees and export your local data.

Current Bill Slabs & Wallet Deductions

Choose 2 or 3 slabs. For each slab, configure the customer commission and the wallet deduction rule.
Wallet deduction = (Bill Amount × Deduction %) + Fixed Extra Deduction. Select the wallet for each slab. Example: 100% and ₹0 means full bill amount is deducted from that wallet.

Credit Card Cash Withdrawal Settings

Set the provider deduction and your profit percentage. The remaining amount is transferred to the customer's account.

Add unlimited amount slabs. Each slab can use different processing deduction, profit percentage and profit wallet. Create additional wallets in Wallets page and select them here.

Customer transfer = Customer Paid − Processing Deduction − Our Profit. Our profit is credited to the wallet selected in the matching slab and counted in Daily / Monthly Profit.

Data & Backup
